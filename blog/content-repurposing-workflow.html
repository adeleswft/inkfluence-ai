The 5-Minute Content Repurposing Workflow

Turn a single blog post into tweets, LinkedIn updates, Instagram captions, and more using this AI-powered workflow.

7 min read · August 2026 · By Inkfluence AI

Writing one great blog post takes hours. But what if that single post could become 10 pieces of social content, an email newsletter, and a LinkedIn article in under 5 minutes?

That is exactly what this AI-powered content repurposing workflow does.

Step 1: Extract the Core Ideas

AI Prompt

Read this blog post: [PASTE FULL POST]. Extract the 5 most valuable insights as a numbered list with one-sentence summaries.

Step 2: Generate Twitter/X Threads

AI Prompt

Turn these 5 insights into a Twitter thread. Hook line first. Each tweet under 280 characters. End with a CTA.

Pro tip: Always ask for 2 variations of each piece. AI outputs improve when you can pick the best version.

Step 3: Create LinkedIn Posts

AI Prompt

Write 3 LinkedIn posts from these insights. Each: hook, brief story/tip, question to drive comments. Under 1,300 characters.

Step 4: Design Instagram Captions

AI Prompt

Create 3 Instagram carousel slides. Slide 1: headline. Slides 2-4: one tip each. Slide 5: CTA. Plus caption with 5 hashtags.

Step 5: Draft an Email Newsletter

AI Prompt

Turn this post into an email newsletter. 3 subject line options, opening hook, 3 key takeaways, CTA. Under 200 words.

Step 6: Create a LinkedIn Article

AI Prompt

Expand into a LinkedIn article. Personal opening, TL;DR at top, discussion question at end. 800-1,200 words.

The Results

Total: 10 platforms, 5 minutes of AI work, zero extra writing.
